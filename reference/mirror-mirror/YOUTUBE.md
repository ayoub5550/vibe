Mirror Mirror — a motion graphics music video made 100% from code.

A harlequin "Jack of Mirrors" dances on the beat across a golden stage built from playing cards, facing his blue reflection. Every lyric line gets its own literal image — the ruler he outgrows, the struck-through "PERFECT", the card storm, the house of cards — ending with a finger touching its reflection on the final word.

No AI images, no stock footage, no 3D models: every pixel is drawn by Three.js + Canvas 2D and rendered frame by frame with HyperFrames, in sync with the song's 150 BPM grid.
