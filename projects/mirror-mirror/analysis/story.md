# Mirror Mirror — what the song says (plain note for the director)

Song: "Mirror Mirror" — Anno Domini Beats (YouTube Audio Library). Excerpt used: 79.22 s, 150 BPM,
beat k at `t = 0.06 + 0.4·k` s. Structure: hook (0–12.86) · verse (12.86–54.46, drums thin until the

## Story in one paragraph
A rapper talks to his own reflection. Fame is a stage full of gold and watching eyes; kings and
queens sell themselves for clicks and deals, one mistake puts you in a cage or a grave, the movie
never ends and the industry crops you out when you slip. He refuses to sell his soul ("Mama
said…"), admits he is not perfect, breaks the strings of a rigged game, and keeps asking the mirror
who will rise and who will fall. The answer is always the same: the truth surfaces.
