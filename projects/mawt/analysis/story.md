# «الموت الهارب» (The Fleeing Death): story note

**Poem:** Tamim Al-Barghouti, recited by the poet in AJ+ ساحة's series «مع تميم» (https://youtu.be/TCTGgQNQpOI,
Content-ID registered). 16 lines, voice 0.54–40.12 s, then a 17 s instrumental outro (no beat: `on_grid` 0.0)
to 57.15 s.

**Metre:** الرجز المشطور (rajaz, one hemistich per line), mono-rhymed on ‑aṣ (ص): an urjūza.
Every line scans as 3 feet of *mustafʿilun* (– – ∪ –) with the usual variants (*mutafʿilun* ∪ – ∪ –,
*mustaʿilun* – ∪ ∪ –), e.g. «رَأَيْـ/ـتُ مَوْ | تي مُقْـ/ـبِلًا | ثُمَّ نَكَصْ» = mutafʿilun | mustafʿilun | mustaʿilun.
So `voice_pulse.py --feet 3`: 72 beats (0.57–1.15 s, mean 0.79 s), every line onset on a beat.

## What the poem says
The speaker sees his death coming, and it **recoils**. The sniper/hunter (قنّاص) is afraid of the hunt. He
follows it through palaces and huts: a gazelle tracking the wolf, seizing its moments of inattention, the
gazelle hungry for the wolf. «Those, by my life, are wonders of stories»: a life that shrinks as it grows, joys
turned into choking griefs. Then he addresses Death directly: none is more cowardly or thievish than you; why did
you skip me in the row where the dead are laid out? Come, I want my share among the shares. The world is upside
down: a monkey dances with the crying boy's bread; the monkey is free and the boy is in the cage. Death flees from
him although it has taken everyone; it is expensive for him in the season when death is cheap.
It is a survivor's poem (the row of the dead, the cheap season of death read as war), bitter and defiant: the
hunter has become the hunted, and the man it spares is the one who calls it.

## Concept: a manga that bursts into anime
One world (a ruined, living Arab city of flat roofs, water tanks, the domed "palaces" and tin huts), one hero
(a young survivor with a long red scarf), one antagonist (Death as a hooded reaper-**sniper**: skull face, red
eye, a sniper rifle with a scythe blade, a Blender prop). The film opens as a **black-and-white manga**:
pages of panels with screentone, hatching and gutters, panel borders drawn in as the lines are spoken, the
world printed in ink. The inverted-world lines play as a manga fable (gazelle and wolf). On «يا موت» (23.16 s),
when the speaker stops running from fate and turns on Death, the page takes an **impact frame**, shatters into
shards, and the film **bursts into colour anime** from the hero's chest outward: cel-shaded three.js city at
dusk, red scarf, aura, speed lines, sound-effect lettering. The instrumental outro is the anime finale: the eye
ignites, a power-up, the hero breaks the boy's cage (the world turned right side up), Death flees into the
sunrise, drops its rifle and scatters into crows; the kite of the lost joy flies again. Title card after the music.
